Lanternreach

A galaxy of sixty star systems, generated from a seed. You have a small ship, a little money and a hold to fill. Trade between ports, take delivery work, hunt pirates, and chart the systems nobody has mapped yet.

To jump, plot a route on the map, then fly outside the dashed jump ring and press JJUMP. Each jump burns one unit of fuel and takes a day. Ships drop to a disabled drift when their hull is badly damaged; fly alongside slowly and press BBOARD to board. Signals on your radar pulse amber: fly to them and press BBOARD to scan.
